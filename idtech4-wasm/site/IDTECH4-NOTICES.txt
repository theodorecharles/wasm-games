idtech4-wasm contains source-built browser engines and no game data.

Pinned sources and exact commits are recorded in /source-lock.json in the
corresponding source repository. Doom 3 engine notices are provided in
/DHEWM3-COPYING.txt. Quake 4 SDK-derived game-module terms and the downstream
engineering record are provided in /QUAKE4-SDK-EULA.rtf and
/QUAKE4-REDISTRIBUTION.md.
Prey engine source notices are provided in /PREY2006-COPYING.txt.

Required Doom 3 data is mounted at /data/base, Resurrection of Evil data at
/data/d3xp, Quake 4 data at /data/q4base, and Prey data at /data/prey/base.
Those files are never part of this site or image layer.
